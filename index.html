Welcome to Sārvatt

🌿

Derived from the ancient Sanskrit word signifying “precious” and “all-pervading,” Sārvatt elevates traditional Gujarati dining into an artful sensory symphony.

A Regal Gastronomic Odyssey from Kathiawar to Surat

Nestled on Ashram Road inside the luxurious Hyatt Regency Ahmedabad, Sārvatt redefines Gujarati cuisine. Stepping away from the conventional overwhelming buffet thali, our culinary philosophy centers on a meticulously curated, pre-plated multi-course dining experience.

Led by acclaimed Master Chef Chandan Parmar, our kitchen weaves the authentic flavours, vibrant aromas, and distinct textures of Gujarat’s culinary heartlands—the rustic earthiness of Kathiawar, the desert richness of Kutch, the delicate sweetness of Surat, and the royal heritage of Saurashtra.

The Culinary Experience

✦

Witness the multi-course pre-plated journey documented in our restaurant reels and photographs.

Course 02

Naashta Pitara

Presented in a dark handcrafted wooden chest with brass latches. Unveil five artisanal ceramic bowls holding golden spiral chakri, standing crisp fafda, spicy gathiya, puffed mamra sev, accompanied by traditional ceramic pickle barnis filled with raw papaya-chili sambharo.

Heirloom Chest Chakri & Fafda Papaya Sambharo
Course 04

Artisanal Chaat Platter

Artfully presented on a hammered copper tray lined with aromatic sun-dried red chilies. Boat-shaped ceramics cradle crisp katori chaat and chilled dahi papdi showered with golden nylon sev, coriander, tangy date-tamarind reduction, and ruby-red pomegranate pearls.

Copper Platter Pomegranate Pearls Nylon Sev
Course 09

Mishtaan Thaal & Tarazu

The crowning finale: an ornate silver-rimmed platter surrounded by dry-ice mist. Features a working miniature golden brass balance scale (Tarazu) balancing regional sweets, alongside berry shrikhand tartlets, warm halwa crowned with edible 24K gold leaf, and pistachio basundi.

Miniature Tarazu 24K Gold Leaf Shrikhand Tartlets

10 Sequential Courses

The Complete Dining Sequence

🌿

Every meal at Sārvatt progresses through a mindful cadence designed to awaken your senses and celebrate Gujarati hospitality.

01

Welcome Beverages

Spiced watermelon cooler rimmed with Gujarati roasted chili salt, followed by a charred citrus orange nectar.

02

Naashta Pitara

Signature wooden chest containing hand-coiled chakri, crisp fafda, gathiya, and raw papaya-chili sambharo.

03

Farsaan Minaar

Multi-tiered silver tower presenting seasonal steamed and crisp savory treats with fresh mint and date chutneys.

04

Artisanal Chaat

Deconstructed street-style chaat in white ceramics resting on a bed of fiery whole sun-dried red chilies.

05

Steam Baksa

Unveiling a traditional brass canister with soft fermented dhokla, delicate khandvi swirls, and mustard seed tadka.

06

Frozen Entremets

A refreshing palate cleanser kulfi popsicle dipped in chilled rabdi syrup to prepare for the main feast.

07

Roti Thaal

Piping-hot millet Rotla topped with fresh white makhan, sweet Puran Poli, golden puffed Puris, and artisanal Khoba Roti.

08

Rice & Khichdi Thaal

Turmeric spiced rice pyramid, warm Gujarati kadhi, kathol chana, and comforting Vaghareli Khichdi poured with golden ghee.

09

Mishtaan Thaal

Regal dessert symphony with miniature brass balance scale, blueberry shrikhand tartlets, 24K gold vark halwa, and badam basundi.

10

Paan Shots

Signature refreshing digestive betel leaf elixir served chilled in individual glass bottles over crushed ice.
